About Us

Naturing Minds since 2010

Our Story

Welcome to Victoria Primary School, where we have been nurturing young minds since 2010. Our dedicated team of educators is committed to providing a high-quality education that fosters academic excellence and personal growth. Victoria Primary School is more than just a place of learning; it is a community where students are encouraged to explore their interests, develop critical thinking skills, and build a strong foundation for lifelong learning. It is our mission to create a safe and inclusive environment where every child can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally. Whether it's through our innovative curriculum, extracurricular activities, or community engagement initiatives, we strive to empower our students to become confident, compassionate, and responsible individuals.
