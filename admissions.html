Welcome to Victoria Primary School Admissions

We are delighted that you are considering Victoria Primary School for your child's education. Our admissions process is designed to ensure that every child has the opportunity to thrive in a supportive and nurturing environment.

Admission Requirements

To apply for admission, please ensure you have the following documents ready:

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate
  • Proof of residence
  • Previous academic records

How to apply

Simple steps to begin

  1. Complete the enquiry form and tell us the age group you are interested in.
  2. Submit the required documents for review by the school office.
  3. Book a school visit and speak with our team before finalizing enrollment.
