Our Programs

At Victoria Primary School, we offer a wide range of academic programs that cater to the diverse needs and interests of our students. Our curriculum is designed to be engaging and relevant, ensuring that every child has the opportunity to succeed.

Language Arts

Our language arts program fosters reading, writing, and communication skills, enabling students to express themselves effectively.

Science

Our science program encourages curiosity and hands-on learning, helping students understand the world around them.

Mathematics

We provide a strong foundation in mathematics, fostering problem-solving skills and logical thinking.

Arts

Our arts program nurtures creativity and self-expression through various mediums, including visual arts, music, and drama.

Physical Education

We promote physical fitness and teamwork through a variety of sports and activities.

Social Studies

Our social studies program helps students understand history, geography, and cultures, fostering global awareness.
